## Description

ForReal puts one quiet line on top of every post in your LinkedIn, X and Bluesky feed, so you can tell in two seconds
whether it deserves your time:

✅ Worth it · Research · Decision Problems in DAO Governance → papers.ssrn.com
🟡 Maybe · Hot take · An opinion, delivered with confidence.
⛔ Skip · Humblebrag · I'm awesome. Read this.

WHAT IT DOES
• A verdict for every post: Worth it, Maybe or Skip, plus the genre (new job, humblebrag, engagement bait, sales pitch,
  AI hype, research, event, launch, hiring, personal news and more).
• Points to the real thing: when a post is about a paper, workshop, tool, dataset or job opening, its link sits right in
  the line.
• Folds only certain junk: ads, engagement bait and near-empty posts fold to one line that still says what they were
  about. One click opens them again. Everything else stays where it is.
• Never hides what matters: posts with a worth-it link and personal news (grief, illness, losing a job) are never folded
  and never joked about.
• Optional jokes: the post's "honest translation", mild, snarky or savage. Switch them off any time.
• "Wrong label?" corrects a verdict and remembers it.
• A small daily report: posts judged, minutes saved, top genres.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Free. No account, no server, no tracking, no ads.
• ForReal's rules run inside your browser. Where Chrome has its built-in on-device AI, that AI may word the two lines;
  it still runs on your computer.
• Nothing about you or the posts you read is sent anywhere, unless you choose to connect your own AI provider in the
  advanced settings (your own key; the text of posts near the screen then goes to that provider only).

Works on linkedin.com, x.com, twitter.com and bsky.app. The extension only reads the feed pages you open; it does not
post, like or message anything.

ForReal is not affiliated with LinkedIn, X or Bluesky.
