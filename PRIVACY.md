# ForReal privacy

ForReal has no server and collects nothing. It reads the posts shown on linkedin.com, x.com, twitter.com and bsky.app
in your browser to judge them.

- **Automatic and Rules only (default):** everything happens on your computer. Chrome's built-in model, when used, runs
  on the device.
- **Your own provider (Claude, OpenRouter, Ollama), only if you choose it:** the text of posts near the screen, the
  author's name and the post's links are sent to that provider with your key, and to nobody else. Its own privacy terms
  apply.
- **Stored on your device only:** settings (synced by your browser if you use browser sync), API keys, daily counts,
  a cache of AI lines (at most 800), and your corrections (at most 300, for 30 days, with the post's text). Remove them in
  Settings or by removing the extension.
