Every post gets one honest line. The fluff folds away, the real stuff gets a → Worth your time link.

Decoder

The local decoder runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. An AI engine writes sharper lines and understands any language; it sends the text of posts you scroll past to the provider you pick.

Start Ollama with OLLAMA_ORIGINS="chrome-extension://*,moz-extension://*" ollama serve so the extension may talk to it.

Keys are stored only on this device (browser extension storage) and sent only to the provider you chose.

Tone

Posts about grief, illness or losing a job are never roasted, at any level.
