One line on every post: ✅ Worth it, 🟡 Maybe or ⛔ Skip. Skipped posts fold away, so your feed gets shorter.

What to fold

By default only certain junk folds away: ads, engagement bait, and posts with almost no signal (below 15). Other Skips keep their label but stay open, so you never miss something you wanted.

Jokes

Posts about grief, illness or losing a job are never joked about.

Judge

Free, and it stays on your computer. ForReal's rules decide the verdict, the genre and the link. Where Chrome has its built-in AI, that AI writes the lines.

Checking Chrome's built-in AI…
Advanced: use your own AI provider

Sharper lines in any language, but the text of posts you scroll past goes to the provider, and you pay for it.

Start Ollama with OLLAMA_ORIGINS="chrome-extension://<this extension's id>" ollama serve (only this extension, not every extension, may then use your Ollama).

Keys are stored only on this device, in storage the feed sites' pages cannot read (Chrome), and sent only to the provider you chose. The AI may re-word and re-label a post, but a sponsored post stays an ad and the link always comes from the rules.

Your corrections

When a label is wrong, click a post's verdict line and pick “Wrong label?”. ForReal remembers it for that post and keeps the example here, on your computer, for 30 days, so the rules can be improved. The examples contain the post's text, i.e. other people's words: share them only with someone you trust.
